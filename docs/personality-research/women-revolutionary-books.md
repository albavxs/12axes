# Revolutionary-left women: book associations

Curated book associations added to the women-leaders expansion.

The runtime distinguishes a work written by or contributed to by the personality
(`associationType: author`) from a biography about the personality
(`associationType: biography`). This prevents a biographer's work from being
presented as if it had been written by the subject.

## Bibliographic references

- Angela Davis — existing project metadata for *Women, Race & Class* (1981).
- Dolores Ibárruri — Akal/Google Books notes the original publication of *El único camino* in exile in 1962.
- Olga Benário — Companhia das Letras and Google Books identify Fernando Morais's *Olga* as first published in 1985.
- Vilma Espín — Pathfinder Press lists *Women in Cuba: The Making of a Revolution Within the Revolution* by Vilma Espín, Asela de los Santos, and Yolanda Ferrer.
- Haydée Santamaría — Google Books lists the 1978 Editorial de Ciencias Sociales edition of *Haydée habla del Moncada*.
- Celia Sánchez — NYU Press/Monthly Review Press lists Nancy Stout's biography, published in 2013.
- Tamara Bunke — Ocean Press/Google Books lists Ulises Estrada's *Tania: Undercover with Che Guevara in Bolivia* (2005).
